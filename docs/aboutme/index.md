### What Does It Mean to Defend an Engineering Decision?

To me, defending an engineering decision means being able to explain why I made a certain choice instead of just saying that it worked. I think it means having calculations, assumptions, requirements, or other evidence that supports the decision and being able to explain why that choice makes more sense than the alternatives. Right now, I understand what it means to defend a decision, but I do not think I fully know how to do it at the level of a practicing engineer yet. I am used to solving problems where there is usually a correct answer, so making a design decision where there could be several possible solutions is something I am still learning. By the end of this course, I hope I am more confident in not only making those decisions, but also explaining and supporting why I made them.
